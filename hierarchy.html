The Hierarchy

How White Rabbit ranks a venue

← Deadlines

Most trackers sort venues into "tier 1" and "tier 2" — borrowed vocabulary that implies a precision nobody actually agrees on. These ranks say the same thing with less false authority, and they come from the book the dashboard is named after.

Queen's Court

The flagship venues. "Off with their heads!" — the Queen's court is where the stakes are highest and the rejection is briskest. Single-digit-to-twenty percent acceptance, the venues a hiring or tenure committee recognises by acronym alone.

OSDI · SOSP · NSDI · FAST · SIGCOMM · SIGMOD · VLDB · CCS · S&P · ICSE

Mad Tea Party

Strong, specialised conferences where the interesting arguments happen. The tea party is never not in session — somebody is always mid-cycle — and the guest list is narrower and more opinionated than the court's. Real peer review, real proceedings, a more focused audience.

NDSS · DSN · ICDCS · IMC · INFOCOM · IPDPS · SoCC · MLSys · SYSTOR · MSST

Caucus Race

Workshops. In Wonderland the caucus race has no start line, no finish line, and everybody wins a prize — which is a fair description of a good workshop. Early ideas, position papers, five pages and an argument. Lower stakes by design, and often where the interesting work shows up first.

HotOS · HotStorage · HotNets · HotInfra · HotCloudPerf · APSys

Looking Glass

Journals. Deliberately outside the pyramid rather than beneath it — they are not a lesser rank, they run on different physics. No deadline, no cycle, no countdown: you submit when the work is ready and the clock never applies. White Rabbit shows them as rolling submission and never counts down.

ACM TOS · ACM TOCS

What this is not

A ranking of research quality. Placement here tracks how a venue behaves — selectivity, breadth of audience, whether it has cycles at all — not how good any particular paper in it is. Plenty of the best work in systems first appeared at a Caucus Race workshop.

It is also one person's judgement call, recorded in one field of conferences.yml. If a venue looks misplaced to you, that is a one-line change and a reasonable pull request.

Ranks live in conferences.yml as tier: — queens-court, tea-party, caucus-race, looking-glass.
