The Hierarchy

How White Rabbit ranks a venue

← Deadlines

Most trackers sort venues into "tier 1" and "tier 2" — borrowed vocabulary that implies a precision nobody actually agrees on. These ranks say the same thing with less false authority. They are poker hands, which suits a dashboard named after a book whose royal court is a literal deck of cards — and unlike "tier 2", nobody needs the ordering explained.

Royal Flush

The best hand in the deck, and the rarest. Single-digit-to-twenty percent acceptance, the venues a hiring or tenure committee recognises by acronym alone. You do not assemble one of these by accident.

OSDI · SOSP · NSDI · FAST · SIGCOMM · SIGMOD · VLDB · CCS · S&P · ICSE

Full House

A strong hand that wins most tables. Specialised conferences with real peer review, real proceedings and a more focused audience — narrower than the Royal Flush venues, and often where the more interesting arguments happen.

NDSS · DSN · ICDCS · IMC · INFOCOM · IPDPS · SoCC · MLSys · SYSTOR · MSST

High Card

You play what you were dealt. Workshops: early ideas, position papers, five pages and an argument. Lower stakes by design — and a High Card played early is often what a Royal Flush is built from two years later.

HotOS · HotStorage · HotNets · HotInfra · HotCloudPerf · APSys

Wild Card

Plays by different rules. Journals have no deadline, no cycle and no countdown: you submit when the work is ready and the clock never applies. That is why the Wild Card sits beside the pyramid rather than beneath it — it is not a weaker hand, it is a different game.

ACM TOS · ACM TOCS

What this is not

A ranking of research quality. Placement here tracks how a venue behaves — selectivity, breadth of audience, whether it has cycles at all — not how good any particular paper in it is. Plenty of the best work in systems first appeared at a Caucus Race workshop.

It is also one person's judgement call, recorded in one field of conferences.yml. If a venue looks misplaced to you, that is a one-line change and a reasonable pull request.

Ranks live in conferences.yml as tier: — royal-flush, full-house, high-card, wild-card.
